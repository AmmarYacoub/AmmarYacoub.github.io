## Course Description & Role
Served as the primary Graduate Teaching Assistant for the **Intro to Mechatronics Laboratory**, guiding undergraduate and graduate students through fundamental hands-on concepts in analog/digital circuit design, sensor instrumentation, actuator control, and embedded systems integration.

## Key Responsibilities & Highlights
* **Lab Experiment Development & Validation:** Supported the end-to-end design, prototyping, and bench-testing of new laboratory modules prior to student deployment, ensuring robust experiment workflows and clear learning objectives.
* **Hands-on Technical Instruction:** Mentored students in circuit assembly, signal acquisition, and real-time control implementation, emphasizing safety, systematic debugging, and measurement accuracy.
* **Student Assessment & Feedback:** Conducted weekly lab sessions, graded technical reports, and provided detailed, actionable feedback to enhance student mastery of hardware-software integration.

## Hardware & Test Equipment Taught
* **Embedded Systems & Microcontrollers:** Arduino (microcontroller programming, I/O interfacing, PWM, sensor reading)
* **Instructional Platforms:** Texas Instruments Analog System Lab Kit (ASLK PRO), Mechatronic Actuators Trainer, Mechatronic Sensors Trainer
* **Benchtop Instrumentation:** Digital Oscilloscopes, Function/Signal Generators, Digital Multimeters (DMMs), Breadboard Prototyping
