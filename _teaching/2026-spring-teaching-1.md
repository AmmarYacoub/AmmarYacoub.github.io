## Course and Role
Taught hands-on laboratory sessions covering system modeling, time-domain response, feedback control, and stability analysis. Guided students as they implemented and tested control algorithms using MATLAB/Simulink and Quanser platforms, including the QUBE-Servo 3 and Aero 2.


## Teaching Contributions
- **Laboratory instruction:** Explained control systems concepts and demonstrated their application during laboratory sessions.
- **Student guidance:** Helped students with modeling, simulations, hardware experiments, and troubleshooting.
- **Grading and feedback:** Graded laboratory assignments and reports and provided feedback to support students’ understanding of the course material.
