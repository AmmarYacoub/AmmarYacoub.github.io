Accurate force control of pneumatic actuators is challenging due to nonlinear and uncertain dynamics arising from friction, external disturbances, air compressibility, and uncertain and time-varying parameters. This paper proposes an adaptive super-twisting sliding mode control (A-ST-SMC) method for force tracking control for a pneumatic actuator. The adaptive mechanism eliminates the need for a priori knowledge of the disturbance bounds while maintaining the robustness properties of sliding mode control. Since piston velocity is not directly measured, a Levant differentiator is incorporated to estimate velocity from position measurements. The proposed controller is developed and experimentally validated using a small, double-acting, single-rod pneumatic cylinder operating under constrained piston motion. Its performance is experimentally compared with a conventional sliding mode controller (SMC) employing a boundary layer to mitigate chattering. The experimental results demonstrate that the proposed A-ST-SMC achieves more accurate force tracking and improved robustness to system uncertainties and external disturbances than the conventional SMC approach.
